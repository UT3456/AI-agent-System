SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days across India and is free on orders above ₹999.
- Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs a flat ₹149.
- Orders are dispatched within 24 hours on business days; dispatch status appears on the order.
- Tracking details are shared by email or SMS once the order ships.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders under ₹5000 with a handling fee of ₹49.
- We do not deliver to PO Boxes or military addresses.

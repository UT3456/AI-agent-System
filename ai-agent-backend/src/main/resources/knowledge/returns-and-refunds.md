RETURNS & REFUNDS POLICY
- Customers can return any eligible item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories and tags.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes quality inspection.
- Shipping costs are only refunded if the return was caused by a wrong or damaged item.
- To start a return, contact support with your order ID; a return label is issued by email.
- Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Clearance or final-sale items are not eligible for returns unless defective.

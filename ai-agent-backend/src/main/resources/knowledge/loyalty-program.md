LOYALTY PROGRAM
- Every order earns loyalty points: 1 point per ₹100 spent.
- Points can be redeemed at checkout: 100 points = ₹10 discount.
- Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.
- Members get early access to sales and a free birthday voucher worth ₹250.
- Elite tier (spend ₹25,000 in a year) unlocks free express shipping on every order.
- Points are credited to the account within 48 hours after the order is delivered.

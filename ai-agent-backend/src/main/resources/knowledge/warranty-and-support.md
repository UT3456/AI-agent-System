WARRANTY & SUPPORT POLICY
- All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the purchase date.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only; physical damage and water damage are excluded.
- For warranty claims, the customer must provide the order ID and a short description of the issue.
- Support is available 7 days a week, 9 AM to 9 PM IST via chat or email.
- Replacement units are shipped within 5-7 business days after the claim is approved.
- Extended warranty plans (additional 1 year) can be purchased within 60 days of the original purchase.
